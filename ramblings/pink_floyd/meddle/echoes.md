# Echoes Part 1 - Meddle by Pink Floyd (1971)

Arguably one of the best and most succesful Pink Floyd songs ever.
Coming off the back of Atom Heart Mother an album that the band did not like even though it was succesful, they wanted to experiment.
They had no direction and no material to work on.
They decided to somewhat revert to a Syd Barret era style music.
Started spending a lot of time perfecting every single sound, and the starting "pings" come from Waters playing a single note through a Leslie speaker, it sounded submarine like.
Leading to verse 1 being set on an ocean:

### Verse 1

```
Overhead the albatross, hangs motionless upon the air.
```

This line possibly is intended to create an image of calm and serene-ness over an ocean. The albatross free as ever, does not feel the need to rush or cause chaos (hangs motionless), and instead chooses to exist in harmony signifying that nature is calm and doesn't need meaning.<br/>
The albatross also may refer to a "concious" mind, where things exist, where the albatross, a thought, is flying, forming. As the mind attempts to find peace it slows itself and the thought is pushed into the background (motionless) which leads nicely to the next line

```
And deep beneath the rolling waves, in labyrinths of coral caves.
```
A stark contrast to the calm and understandable albatross above the sea, we head below the ocean into a place we can barely understand (labyrinths), something so deep we cannot comprehend it. Yet again this could refer to the fact that nature has harmony in it's simplicity (line 1) and complexity (line 2), or more so as I believe, 
<br/>it refers to the unconcious mind. As the albatross was pushed into the background to find peace, the mind goes silent, and enters a state that it doesn't know, but which is peaceful, where it has no thoughts and no control, no albatross, no motion, just beauty that cannot be comprehended by the concious, but only retrieved in portions through our subconcious, yet again leading into the next line.

```
The echo of a distant time, came willowing across the sand.
```

In our nature sense, this could refer to the fact that nature and life has far reaching consequences that while affecting everything are barely noticable (an echo). The effects persists despite the source being long gone, but it's power keeps reducing despite it's importance remaining constant. The present is built on a past that while forgotten, unimaginably ancient has shaped everything i.e. nature has always been and always will be. <br/>
Or... it could mean that the echoes here refer to a faint memory of the unconcious mind, the ever complex beauty, (distant time), coming through the subconcious (came... across the sand) to the concious, not a complete memory in it's entirety but an echo that reminds of what can be, what is, what exists inside.

```
And everything is green and submarine.
```

The green and submarine here in the nature sense attempts to invoke a feeling of an alien world, underwater filled with vegetation, something that is familiar when split but incomprehensible when together. Like nature, we can understand individual components but understanding all of nature at once is not something that is feasible. Once again solidifying nature is above mortals. 
<br/>Or it refers to the fact that the echo that was delivered to the mind was truly of the unconcious, refering to the underwater (submarine) and coral caves (green).

> All in all this paragraph either: <br/> a) conveys the idea	that nature is ever lasting, doesn't require humans, and is not understandable in it's full extent by us. <br/>b) talks about the three states of mind and how they interact with each other.


### Chorus 1

The chorus with Wright's vocals is haunting and calming at the same time.

```
And no one showed us to the land, and no one knows the where's or why's.
```

This either talks about evolution literally, that no one handed us a guide on how to evolve or what to do and that again nature does what nature does and we dont know what or how or why it does (no one knows the where's or why's). 

Or it could refer to existence as a whole, individually and collectively, no one ever told us what to do, there is no user handbook on how to live life, we don't know why we are here, or what even here means.

```
But something stirs and something tries, and starts to climb towards the light.
```

In the evolutionary sense this could mean that nature keeps trying things till evolution to a human was done, as complex life emerged from simple unicellular life. It refers to repeated trial and error for small gains to reach the end goal (light). 

> Or it could refer to the fact that despite not knowing anything, humans try their best to be curious and know stuff, whether for good or bad we can only assume. We don't need an in-built purpose to discover things and have goals and feelings. And eventually in that process we make progress on finding our purpose and meaning. We develop feelings, a will to live, to grow, to reach beyond our limitations.

### Verse 2

This verse suddenly shifts from the emergence of curiosity to what happens after we reach "the light". Both interpretations lead to human concious being developed at this stage.

```
Strangers passing in the street, by chance to seperate glances meet.
```

The song moves from grandiose events like creation and evolution to a mundane daily encounter, hoping to understand it. It also contrasts the albatross from line one, that was motionless, alone, and peaceful, together with that this is supposed to imply, humans somewhere deviated from "the light", and got more invested in other ventures. The glances meeting is accidental, unplanned, but it makes both of them recognise the other exists, which will seemingly a mundane observation if given though to turns out to be profound in the revelation that others are just as concious as you, and the larger idea of what self means.

```
And I am you and what I see is me.
```

Continuing on the previous theme, the strangers start thinking about the idea of self and arrive at the fact that everyone is fundamentally the same, a human, with a concious mind, who shares the same world history with them, both of them have feelings just as real as the other's. A deeper analysis could get you thinking about the fact that for another everything in their vision exists solely due to them (what I see is me), as in, once they are not observing it, for them it ceases to exist, so what is to say you aren't just a part of someone else's vision, this struggle of identity in an ever-evolving yet permanent world is the core idea of the song.

```
And do I take you by the hand, and lead you through the land, and help me understand the best I can.
```

Wonder sets in amongst both on reflection as to the fact they do not understand their own existence, as recognising the other, they think if they should reach out (take by the hand), and explore the existence together (lead through the land), and help each other understand themselves more in the process.

> This Verse explores the idea of discovering human relationships and how they answer questions we have no ways of even asking. There is also a quaint acceptance of the fact that they will never truly understand anything or everything but they will try the best they can. By understanding others is only how we truly understand ourselves.

### Chorus 2

```
And no one calls us to move on, and no one forces down our eyes.
```

Instead of Chorus 1, where the start of existence was being discussed, here the continuation of the existence is discussed.
If no one is guiding us, to move on, to feel something, to confront reality, then how and why do we do anything without guidance. Or going one level further maybe it is only absence of coercion that makes recogition and connection possible, and if we postulate understanding is neccessary for long term existence, then we have already established connection is required for understanding. But this could also lead to a doom where we grow complacent due to the lack of guidance.

```
No one speaks and no one tries, and no one flies around the Sun.
```

The interpretation of Chorus 2 completely differs based on your worldview, if you believe humans need guidance this could refer to the fact that we do nothing, isolate ourselves (no one tries), and stop exploring (no one flies...). Or it could mean the nature doesnt speak or try to do anything, despite our best efforts we cannot extract meaning from nature. Or it just means that there is no creator, no god speaking or trying to guide us, and maybe not flying around the sun refers back to the idea of "What I see is me", maybe we dont exist, maybe the Earth doesnt go around the Sun, maybe nothing exists, maybe we are just an "echo of a distant time", trying our best to hold on to memories we cannot keep in a world we cannot recognise or understand. Uncertainity invites freedom but also doubt. The question of existence is left open ended in this way, with everyone able to extract their own meaning from the song.
