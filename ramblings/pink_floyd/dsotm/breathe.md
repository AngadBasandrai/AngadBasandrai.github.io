# Breathe (In the Air)

Starting with his biggest insecurities and fears, the song "breathes" life into the album.

The intro guitar solo is supposed to introduce a feeling of a calmness inside a mad world. The "elongated" chords bring a vibe of eery haunting that follows in the lyrics.

This is further exemplified when the narrator starts with

```
Breathe, Breathe in the air
```
Simply telling you to let free of the burdens and the pains of this world, by breathing it out. Maybe signalling to the fact that its genuinely all just in your head. Maybe I am reading too deep into this simple line but this is the core of the song in my opinion, breathing in the air here in my opinion is a symbol of freedom in a world focused on constricting you and your aims. You breathe into the air, leaving the burdens and the pressure behind and be yourself, focus on what you want, connect with what you choose and exist. The listener is being begged to experience existence.

```
Don't be afraid to care
```
The narrator after looking at his own life realising that he has just been working tirelessly not for his own goals, but for others, tells the listener to care about themselves, and their own goals and dreams.
```
Leave, but don't leave me.
Look around, choose your own ground
```
The first line is a plea from the narrator to leave the bonds of this world to let yourself be and exist for yourself, and a request, nay more of begging to not leave him, as we have established in his working he has become "mad" and we can extrapolate a sense of lonliness throughout this album, and it seems to try to impart some sort of wisdom onto the listener. This also works as advice to not abandon the people and things you care about while going on "your own ground".

The second line ask the listener yet again to be free, not to sound like a broken record, but it is a request, from the narrator's experience, that the listener doesn't make his mistakes and chooses his own decisions and be not trapped and go "mad". The listener is told to find their own meaning, in an almost Camus-esque way.
```
Long you live and high you fly
```
A simple but deep line, it hopes for you to live a long life and achieve your ambitions, something most people fail to do despite it being everyone's goal.
```
Smiles you'll give and tears you'll cry
And all you touch and all you see
Is all your life will ever be
```
These three lines have a simple meaning that tie the whole thread together. All your experiences are your entire existence, there is nothing outside them, no greater meaning, and hence, the pleading to "breathe" and "care" deepen. Because those events define your life, they hold more weight. Not to delve into a rabbithole of philosophy here, but this is similar to what I believe Nietzsche meant in his book "Thus Spoke Zaruthustra" when he mentioned "God is dead..." and hence developed the idea of "Übermensch", where on the death of "God" (i.e. death of implicit meaning), the "Super" man derives his own meaning from his existence and experience or in a broader sense what Epicurus' "quality of life" defines meanning means. All in all, this is to signify that even the most trivial act in your life, IS A PART OF YOUR LIFE, so experience it, dont lose it, like the narrator who spent his life "working for bands", going mad.

This is immediately contrasted with a change of harmony from an E-major-esque tone to a G-minor-tone as it heads into a criticism of humans and maybe in some sense, the narrator's resentment towards his own life.
```
Run, rabbit, run
```
The rabbit simply refers to the ordinary human and running refers to going through life, and while this line doesn't require a section of it's own lyrically, musically it is the biggest change in the song, which goes from advice to criticism, it goes from a lesson from the narrator's life to a cry for help from the narrator, for himself to you, and for you to himself. The change in the guitar signifies the shift in tone, which powerful in itself, is amplified when the drums shift to double-time pulse instead of a simple 4/4 beat which signify the narrator becoming impatient or anxious or potentially nervous as he starts talking about his own resentments with himself, a topic no one feels comfortable talking about.
```
Dig that hole, forget the sun
```
Digging the hole refers to working, and forgetting the sun simply means forgetting everything outside work. This is the narrator defining his own life and the mistakes most people make.
```
When, at last, the work is done
Don't sit down, it's time to dig another one
```
The narrator doubles down on his criticism for the "way of life" of working without end as he talks about how modern life is just an endless cycle of digging a hole, completing it, and immediately starting digging another one (work -> achieve -> more work -> ...). The self made promise of, "If I work hard now, I will enjoy later", never truly materializes as you get stuck in an endless vicious cycle.

The key shifts somewhat back to the old E-centered tune as even though it return even lyrically to almost the same place as before, the meaning now is totally different.
```
Long you live and high you fly
```
Giving a hint of a familiar statement before arguably the largest reality check of the entire song.
```
But only if you ride the tide
Balanced on the biggest wave,
You race towards an early grave.
```
You can live long and achieve all you want, if you ride the tide, i.e. go with the flow, take life as it comes, and enjoy it safely chasing happiness and your personal goals over "success".

Whereas someone who is "balanced on the biggest wave" may feel like they are suceeding and doing better than everyone, but they are just shortening their life be it in terms of physically experiencing stress and more issue or be it due to "effective time" as someone caught up all the time in work and chasing success ("biggest wave"), has no time left for self fulfillment. This is where we talk about Søren Kierkegaard, who explictly begs the reader to "be themselves" and not lose themselves in optimising for metrics chosen by others, suceed in your own way, as the masses are often misled and untrue. In his wn words "The crowd is untruth." and "The most common form of despair is not being who you are". This last verse is some regards is referring to the same idea where the narrator tells the listener that riding the "biggest wave" will lead to nothing but losing yourself and hence effectively dying while living for someone else. I venture further that the narrator would like for the listener to go a level deeper and wonder what living truly means, is it, What you have? What you had? What you think of others? What others think of you? or maybe as Schopenhauer argues, it is all about, What you are, or mainly "What a man is by himself, what accompanies him when he is alone, what no one can give him or take away, is evidently more essential to him than everything he possesses in the way of property, or even what he may be in the eyes of the world". I will take my liberties and go even further to say Pink Floyd's true meaning was even more elegant and simpler and yet profoundly deeper, in what you experience is decided by you, and hence your existence is fully yours, so why let it be shaped by other's metrics? Why ride the biggest wave, or again as Schopenhauer puts it, "The world in which a man lives shapes itself chiefly by the way in which he looks at it".


<h3> Deeper connections to the album </h3>
This song serves as the link that all future songs refer to and build upon in this album, deceptively simple, in it's two minutes it conveys far more than most of us can convey in hours. It builds the premise of existence, the why, the how, the how not to, the motivations of the narrator, and the mental state of the listener and the narrator.

## My short interpretation of the song as part of a thought process that the album is
Once the person goes sleep this song is what he is thinking about, about his existence, realising what his life is, what he wants it to be, and what he needs to do.
