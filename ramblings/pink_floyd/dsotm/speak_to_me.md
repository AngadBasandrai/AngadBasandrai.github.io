# Speak to Me

Just a segue into the album, making clear the narrator is a guy working for bands, going mad with his work, and this album is his gift to the world.

## My short interpretation of the song as part of a thought process that the album is
This song functions as the opening scene, where a person is just lying on the bed thinking of his life in solitary, and as he slowly drifts in and out of sleep the song ends.
